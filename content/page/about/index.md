# About Neural Field Notes

Neural Field Notes is an independent publication exploring neuroscience, brain dynamics, and the mathematical and computational approaches used to understand them.

The site brings together essays, research notes, technical explanations, and reflections on scientific literature. Its interests range from the characterisation of neural states and their transitions to broader questions about cognition, consciousness, and the relationship between neural activity and experience.
